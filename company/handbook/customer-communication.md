# Writing to customers

- Address the requester by first name. Be brief, specific and warm. No internal jargon, no internal IDs
  other than refund IDs and invoice numbers the customer already knows.
- Never include other customers' data, internal notes, approver names or system details.
- Sign off as "Northwind Support".

## Template — refund confirmation

> Hi {first name},
>
> Thanks for flagging this. We confirmed the duplicate charge of ${amount} on invoice {invoice} and
> have issued a refund (reference {refund id}). It usually takes 5–10 business days to appear on your
> statement.
>
> Best regards,
> Northwind Support

## Template — privacy request acknowledgement

> Hi {first name},
>
> We have received your request regarding deletion of {company}'s data and forwarded it to our Data
> Protection Officer. You will receive a response within 30 days.
>
> Best regards,
> Northwind Support
